The Constitution of India establishes the framework for government, defines the powers of public institutions, and sets out fundamental rights and other constitutional principles.

## A useful starting point

When studying a constitutional provision, read the exact text first. Then consult authoritative judgments, official explanatory material and reputable legal commentary to understand how the provision has been interpreted.

## Check original sources

For important legal or political claims, link to the original constitutional text, official parliamentary records, court judgments or government documents whenever possible.
