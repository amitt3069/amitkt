# Coalition politics is back — but the rules of power are changing

The politics of coalition has returned in a new form. Regional parties are not only negotiating for parliamentary seats, they are redefining what genuine bargaining power looks like in a fragmented national environment.

The result is a changed politics of compromise. It is no longer enough to outvote the opposition; it is about making alliances durable and sustainable.

## The strategic shift

In the current environment, state-level politics are increasingly shaping national narratives. This creates a new challenge for national parties: they can no longer assume the center has a single meaning.

Instead, they must navigate a layered political map where ideological compatibility is often less important than local accountability and delivery.

The winners are not necessarily the loudest voices. They are the ones best positioned to coordinate diverse interests without losing political coherence.
