# Digital identity and politics: where surveillance and citizenship meet

The story of digital identity is increasingly political. Governments and private platforms are mapping identification, access, and monitoring through systems that promise convenience but also produce new imbalance in power.

For citizens, the issue is not simply whether an app works. It is whether the state and technology together are widening inclusion or only increasing surveillance.

## Why it matters

In a country as large and diverse as India, digital identity is a tool of governance, but also a test of democratic fairness. If the systems are opaque, exclusionary, or poorly designed, they risk turning access into a new kind of gatekeeping.

The debate must consider not just administrative efficiency, but also the rights of citizens who do not always fit neatly into digital frameworks.
