# The farm law debate is not only about income — it is about trust

Any serious conversation about the farm law debate needs to move beyond numbers and subsidies. The issue has become a question of institutional trust.

Farmers are not only arguing about prices or procurement. They are asking whether policy can be designed with their participation and whether governance is listening before it acts.

## Why this matters

When citizens stop trusting institutions, even economically sound proposals can become politically explosive. The debate has therefore moved from economics to legitimacy.

This is especially important in a democratic system where the voice of the rural electorate remains central to the national balance of power.

For policy to work, it must be not only efficient but also believable.
